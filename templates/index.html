About OmeBox: private, no‑login data transfer

OmeBox is a lightweight data exchange platform built with Flask. Use it to transfer files, notes, or lab copy snippets without login, without signup. Perfect for moving data between PC, Mac, iPhone, Android in seconds — just like sharedrop or sharebox.

Privacy policy: no tracking · no email

✅ No personal data collected. OmeBox does not require login, email, or phone. We do not use trackers, analytics, or cookies for marketing. Your shared content is temporary (auto‑expiry) and never sold or inspected.

✅ No tracking numbers, no fingerprinting. Because there’s no account, we cannot link transfers to any identity. Perfect for sensitive context sharing.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This is for educational purpose. Avoid sharing highly sensitive information. Data transfer is temporary but not encrypted end‑to‑end (use for non‑critical items). By using OmeBox you agree to the “as‑is” nature.
